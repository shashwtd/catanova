# Quiet coastal lounge

Original environment regenerated with the built-in image generation tool for the signed-in hub and waiting room. The second version keeps the coastal veranda direction but removes the town, boats, banners, furniture and dense greenery. The public landing and game board retain their artwork.

Runtime: `apps/client/public/art/optimized/quiet-lounge.336ba3af237d.webp`, 1600 × 900, 53,072 bytes (about 52 KiB). Sharp resized the result and encoded WebP at quality 76, effort 6. Only this optimized, content-hashed file is shipped. The original generation PNG remains outside the repository. This replaces the 91,832-byte coastal-lounge texture.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original wide 16:9 background for Catanova fantasy board-game waiting lobby. Regenerate the coastal lodge direction as a much calmer, simpler hand-painted environment: an open shaded timber veranda, quiet teal sea and two broad soft distant hills, warm afternoon light. Just one broad timber beam in each far upper corner, a single small warm lantern at the far left. Lower third is shaded smooth walnut flooring, middle is muted teal-green open space with generous negative space for player cards. No rug, furniture, pots, banners, vines, town, harbor buildings, boats, props or tiny decorative objects. Confident broad gouache brushwork, soft atmospheric distance, crafted animation-background feel, friendly restrained colors, natural light, a few intentional silhouettes, readable when cropped to portrait. No text, logos, characters, interface, watermarks, photorealism, shiny 3D, neon or excessive detail. Reference image is style/direction guidance only; simplify the scene substantially.

Reference: the previous original coastal-lounge background. The result retains a subdued floor inset, but the much simpler shoreline and framing achieve the requested reduction in visual clutter.

The small `art/ui/lobby-crest.svg` is original code-drawn decorative brass linework, not generated raster art. Existing character illustrations are preserved; their lobby portraits are larger, with double inset medallions and a larger host crown.
