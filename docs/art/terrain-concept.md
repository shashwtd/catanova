# Terrain and ocean concept — preview only

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These assets are intentionally under `apps/client/src/dev/assets`, not the public asset folder. Only the development-only lounge preview imports them; production continues to use the existing artwork. Default preview uses the existing art until the user enables **Preview → Concept terrain & ocean**. Disable it to compare the same board and pieces.

- `terrain-concept.webp`: 1536 × 1024, 172,232 bytes; six equal cells, three columns/two rows.
- `environment-concept.webp`: 1024 × 1024, 39,596 bytes; two columns/two rows.
- Both resized to their atlas geometry and compressed with Sharp, WebP quality 78, effort 6. Full-size originals remain outside the repository.
- The concept also narrows terrain interiors by 1.5 world units, exposing a little more of the light sandy road network. It does not change build rules or legal placements.
- The first ocean attempt retained too much fine wave detail; the second attempt below is the selected version. It was generated fresh rather than reusing the noisy atlas.

## Terrain prompt

Create a production concept terrain texture atlas for Catanova. Exactly 3 columns and 2 rows of equal square cells, edge-to-edge with NO gutters, labels or borders. 1536x1024 landscape. Reference image is the existing atlas; keep EXACT layout and unmistakable identity but reduce visual clutter by half. Top left Timber: a few broad rounded evergreen canopies and two warm trunks with a clear mossy clearing. Top middle Clay: terracotta clay bluffs with broad soft planes, one open quarry basin. Top right Sheep: soft green pasture and two woolly sheep, no fences or tiny flowers. Bottom left Hay: golden fields in broad swathes, one hay sheaf. Bottom middle Rock: two blue-grey rock outcrops, clear slate ground, no many little pebbles or trees. Bottom right Desert: a few warm sand dunes, no props. Camera elevated near overhead for a board game. Human-painted gouache animation background, bold readable medium-sized forms, gentle depth and soft cast shadows, warm inviting selective texture, restrained contrasts, medium-light palette, not shiny 3D or photoreal. Keep edges calmer and lighter than centers so sandy road corridors can read cleanly after hex cropping. Avoid flat polygon art, icon-only terrain, dense detail, sharp black shadows, text, watermarks, perspective horizons. Each square should read at tiny board scale and each cell must have exactly square proportions.

## Selected ocean prompt

Generate a NEW restrained hand-painted game texture atlas, 1024x1024, 2x2 equal square cells, exact edge-to-edge quadrants without dividers, text or margin. Do NOT copy the fine detail of the reference. Main change: make the water genuinely calm and broad. TOP LEFT: deep blue-teal water like a sheltered lagoon on a still day, only 3 or 4 broad soft ripples across the entire square, smooth continuous painted depth underneath; no breaking waves, no white foam, no dense brush dabs. TOP RIGHT: calm pale turquoise shallow lagoon; 3 or 4 very broad softly blurred patches of sunlight under the water, no sharp caustic web, no froth, no rippling glitter. BOTTOM LEFT: pale cream-beige sand, almost smooth with subtle soft paper grain, at most 4 very small pebbles. BOTTOM RIGHT: muted walnut, softly painted broad horizontal grain. All materials should have smooth low-frequency modeling, subtle painterly paper texture and depth, with about 80 percent quiet unbroken areas. Not flat vector colors or polygons: softly varied value and color with visible yet quiet brushwork. No coastlines, horizon, props, harsh shapes, pronounced repeated wave pattern, text, logos, frames. This is an artist-painted fantasy board game material, intended behind small gameplay pieces. Restrained texture, not noisy illustration.
